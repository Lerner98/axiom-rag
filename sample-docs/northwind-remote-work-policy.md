# Northwind Robotics - Remote Work Policy (fictional sample document)

*Version 3.2, effective March 1, 2026. Owner: People Operations.*

## 1. Eligibility

Employees who have completed their 90-day probation period may work remotely
up to three days per week. Engineering staff on the Platform team may work fully
remote with written approval from their director. Lab technicians and hardware
assembly staff are not eligible for remote work because their duties require
on-site equipment.

## 2. Core hours

All remote employees must be reachable between 10:00 and 15:00 Central European
Time (CET). Outside core hours, employees may set their own schedule as long as
they work 40 hours per week.

## 3. Equipment and stipend

Northwind provides a laptop, a 27-inch monitor and a headset. Remote employees
receive a one-time home-office stipend of 600 EUR, plus 45 EUR per month toward
internet costs. Receipts must be submitted through the Expensa portal within
30 days of purchase.

## 4. Security requirements

- Company laptops must use full-disk encryption (BitLocker) at all times.
- The corporate VPN is required when accessing the source code repository,
  the customer database, or the HR system.
- Public Wi-Fi may only be used together with the VPN.
- Lost or stolen devices must be reported to security@northwind.example within
  4 hours.

## 5. Travel to the office

Remote employees who live more than 150 km from the Tel Aviv office are expected
to visit for the quarterly planning week. Travel and hotel costs for these
visits are reimbursed at the standard business-travel rate.

## 6. Performance and review

Remote work arrangements are reviewed every six months by the employee's
manager. An arrangement can be paused if two consecutive quarterly goals are
missed, after a written improvement plan of at least 30 days.

## 7. Contacts

Questions about this policy go to People Operations (people@northwind.example).
Questions about equipment go to IT Support, extension 4410.
